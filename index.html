📋 The 12 Cleaning Steps I Performed

01

Protected Raw Data

Duplicated the sheet and renamed original to RAW_DATA before touching anything.

02

Removed Duplicates

Found 12 red highlighted cells (6 duplicate rows). Used Data → Remove Duplicates. Left with 100 unique rows.

03

Standardised Department

Fixed 'engineering', 'SALES', 'Hr' → clean values using formula then Find & Replace.

=PROPER(TRIM(D2))
04

Cleaned Annual Salary

Removed $ signs and commas from salary column, converted text to real numbers.

=VALUE(SUBSTITUTE(SUBSTITUTE(G2,",",""),"$",""))
05

Removed Negative Salaries

Blanked out impossible negative salary values — can't pay someone minus money.

=IF(G2<0,"",G2)
06

Fixed Invalid Ages

Removed ages below 18 and above 70 (impossible for an employee). Found -3 and 999 values.

=IF(OR(C2<18,C2>70),"",C2)
07

Fixed Years Experience

Removed negative experience values — you can't have -2 years of experience.

=IF(OR(H2<0,H2>50),"",H2)
08

Standardised Education & Job Level

Fixed 'bachelor', 'PHD', 'SENIOR', 'junior' → consistent casing across both columns.

=PROPER(TRIM(E2))
09

Standardised Attrition Column

Mapped YES/yes/1 → "Yes" and NO/no/0 → "No" using multi-condition IFS formula.

=IFS(OR(K2="yes",K2="1"),"Yes",OR(K2="no",K2="0"),"No",TRUE,K2)
10

Fixed Performance Rating

Scale is 1–5. Blanked out values of 0 and 6 which were clearly data entry errors.

=IF(OR(I2<1,I2>5),"",I2)
11

Fixed Job Satisfaction

Same 1–5 scale. Removed invalid value of 6 found in multiple rows.

=IF(OR(J2<1,J2>5),"",J2)
12

Standardised Hire Dates

4 different date formats (YYYY-MM-DD, MM/DD/YYYY, DD-Mon-YYYY, YYYY/MM/DD) all converted to uniform format.

=IFERROR(DATEVALUE(B2),IFERROR(DATEVALUE(SUBSTITUTE(B2,"/","-")),B2))
